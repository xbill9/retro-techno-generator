Me, stanning a DEV challenge again? Apparently yes. This time I handed the drum machine to my own body. 🎛️

My entry for the Presage SmartSpectra SDK Challenge on @DEV Community is Retro Techno Generator: a webcam reads your breathing, pulse and facial expressions through the SmartSpectra SDK, and a browser synth turns them into 90s acid techno and early-90s hardcore. No samples, every sound is synthesised in Web Audio.

What it does:

- Breathing, pulse, smile, surprise, blink and talking each play a part of the track
- Dancing opens the filters and doubles the hi-hats; freeze for a few seconds and it re-reads your vitals
- A mirror view with plain instructions shows you where to sit, with a Pixelate button so you can stream or record without showing your face
- The camera only starts after a consent screen, nothing is stored, and SDK telemetry is off
