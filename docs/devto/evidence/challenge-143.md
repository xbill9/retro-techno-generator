 The Presage SmartSpectra SDK Challenge runs November 4 to November 22. Build an experience that responds to real-time human signals captured through a camera, such as pulse, breathing rate, HRV, or expressions. $3,000 in prizes across ten winners.

New to Presage? The SmartSpectra SDK lets your app capture real-time human signals through a camera, so you can build experiences that respond to the people using them.

Our Prompt
Best Overall Human-Sensing Experience

Build a working experience with the Presage SmartSpectra SDK that responds meaningfully to real-time human signals captured through a camera.

Prize Categories

Two prize categories are open to any submission that fits. You don't need to enter a prize category to be eligible for an overall winner spot. If you're entering one, note it in the Prize Categories section of the submission template.

    Most Creative or Delightful Use: the most original, playful, or unexpected use of SmartSpectra
    Best Thoughtful Real-World Use: SmartSpectra applied to a clear real-world need with strong usability, consent, and responsible data handling

Your submission can be eligible for an overall winner spot and both prize categories at once, but each participant may only submit one entry, and each submission can win only once.

Submission Requirements 📌

Each participant may submit one entry. Team submissions are not allowed, and participants must be 18+.

Every submission must:

    Integrate the SmartSpectra SDK into a functional app and use at least one live Presage signal, such as pulse, breathing rate, HRV, or expressions
    Include a DEV post, public code repository, setup instructions, and demo video (public hosting is optional)
    Explain camera consent and data handling, and disclose any storage, sharing, retention, deletion, SDK telemetry, or optional AI service use

Judges will verify SmartSpectra SDK use by reviewing your public code repository and demo video.

Not allowed: diagnosis or treatment claims, medical decision-making, or covert or non-consensual monitoring.

Judging Criteria

All submissions will be evaluated for:

    Use of underlying technology
    Usability and user experience
    Originality and creativity

Prizes 🏆

Four overall winners from the "Best Overall Human-Sensing Experience" prompt, three winners from the "Most Creative or Delightful Use" prize category, and three winners from the "Best Thoughtful Real-World Use" prize category will each receive:

    $300 USD cash prize
    DEV++ Membership
    Exclusive DEV Badge

All participants with a valid submission will receive a completion badge on their DEV profile.

How To Participate

Publish a post on DEV using the submission template and be sure to include the required challenge tag: #presagechallenge.

If your app requires logging in, please provide testing credentials in your submission and/or instructions on how to best test your application for judges.

This challenge runs on Presage's free trial, with no extra credits provided. If you hit a limit, reach out on the Presage Discord.

    November 22: Submissions due at 11:59 PM PST
    December 10: Winners Announced
