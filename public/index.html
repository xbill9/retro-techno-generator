RETRO TECHNO GENERATOR

Your pulse sets the tempo and picks the style: ambient when calm, acid techno in between, early-90s hardcore when it's racing. Dance and the filters open and the hi-hats double; freeze for a few seconds and it re-reads your pulse, so a hard dance pushes the tempo up. Smiles, blinks, talking and breathing play the rest.

Pressing Start turns on your camera so the SmartSpectra SDK can measure pulse, breathing and expressions. A small mirror view helps you line up; it stays on this computer and the video is never recorded or stored. Nothing is saved. SDK telemetry is switched off. Measurements need a session with Presage's servers; see the README.

This is a toy, not a medical device.
