RETRO TECHNO GENERATOR

Your pulse sets the tempo and picks the style: ambient when calm, acid techno in between, early-90s hardcore when it's racing. Smiles, blinks, talking and breathing play the rest.

Pressing Start turns on your camera so the SmartSpectra SDK can measure pulse, breathing and expressions. The video is never displayed, recorded or stored. Only the numbers reach this page, and nothing is saved. SDK telemetry is switched off. Measurements need a session with Presage's servers; see the README.

This is a toy, not a medical device.
